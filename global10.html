A greener school

Bài nói mẫu ngắn bằng tiếng Anh về chủ đề bảo vệ môi trường.

🌿 Humans and the Environment

Speaking sample · A2–B1

How can we make our school greener?

Good morning, everyone. Today, I would like to talk about how to make our school greener. First, we should put rubbish in the correct bins and recycle paper and plastic bottles. Second, students can bring reusable water bottles instead of buying single-use plastic bottles. We can also plant more trees and keep our classrooms clean. These small actions are easy to do, but they can make a big difference. If everyone takes action, our school will become a cleaner and healthier place. Thank you for listening!
