03 · SPEAKING PRACTICE

A sample mini speech

Small actions, greener planet 🌱

Hello everyone. Today, I would like to talk about protecting the environment. Our planet faces problems such as air pollution and plastic waste. However, we can all make a difference through small actions. For example, we can bring reusable bottles, sort our rubbish and save electricity. We should also encourage our friends and family to care for nature. If everyone takes responsibility, our community will become cleaner and healthier. Thank you for listening!
